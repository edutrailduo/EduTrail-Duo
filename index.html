ABOUT EDU TRAIL DUO

Two friends. One shared journey.

EduTrail Duo is a platform where friendship, education, travel and discovery come together. Founded by two friends pursuing bachelor’s degrees in South Korea, we share practical information, personal experiences and stories that encourage people to learn beyond boundaries and explore the world.

From university admissions, scholarships and visa information to student life and travel vlogs, our aim is to make useful knowledge easier to access. We distinguish personal experience from official requirements and link to authoritative sources whenever possible.
